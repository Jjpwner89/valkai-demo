# Template — the four PBRER sections

Follow this structure. Keep every claim traceable to a source; where the data don't support a statement, say so rather than inferring it.

## Section 1 — Executive Summary

A structured overview. Include:

- Active substance, therapeutic class / ATC, formulation(s) and strength(s), brand name(s).
- Marketing authorisation holder(s).
- International Birth Date (IBD), EU Reference Date (EURD), report number, reporting interval.
- Worldwide MA status in one line (number of countries authorised / marketed).
- Regulatory highlights this interval (new authorisations; withdrawals or status changes; actions taken for safety reasons).
- Estimated exposure this interval and cumulatively.
- Adverse reactions this interval and cumulatively (serious / non-serious).
- One-line overall benefit-risk statement.

## Section 2 — Worldwide Marketing Authorisation Status + Actions Taken for Safety Reasons

- Summary of where the product is authorised and marketed (counts + representative list).
- New authorisations in the interval.
- Any withdrawals, suspensions, or status changes — and whether for safety reasons.
- Actions taken in the interval for safety reasons (by the MAH or a health authority), or an explicit statement that none were.

## Section 3 — Estimated Exposure

- Interval patient exposure from marketing experience (with method / assumptions stated).
- Cumulative patient exposure (state how it was derived).
- Clinical-trial exposure if provided.
- Flag explicitly any region or figure that is missing, estimated, or caveated.

## Section 4 — Signal & Risk Evaluation + Benefit-Risk Conclusion

For the safety topic named in the brief:

1. **Risk and current status** — the risk and its status (identified / potential) per the RSI; current risk minimisation.
2. **New information in the interval** — serious cases with onset in the interval; key features; any fatal or notable cases.
3. **Cumulative experience** — cumulative serious case count and pattern; how the interval compares.
4. **Reporting rate** — computed from the exposure in Section 3 if possible; state clearly if it cannot be.
5. **Evaluation** — what the interval data mean; anything new or changed.
6. **Benefit-risk conclusion** — the effect (if any) on the benefit-risk balance, and any proposed action or a reasoned statement that none is warranted.
